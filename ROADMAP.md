# Roadmap

## Next up
- **Accounts with cloud storage**: Google sign-in exists today but decks live in the browser.
  Add a backend so decks follow you between devices and share links can be short URLs with images.
- **Google Slides link import**: paste a Slides URL instead of downloading a .pptx
  (needs the Google Drive API and an OAuth scope).
- **Better PPTX fidelity**: grouped shapes, charts as images, slide backgrounds, more than one image.
- **Server-side video export**: render MP4 without the screen-share prompt.
- **Low-motion view**: a calmer fallback for people who prefer less movement
  (the player already respects the system "reduce motion" setting).

## More styles
- 🌊 **Ocean dive**: sink from the surface to the sea floor; deeper = more detail.
- 🌦️ **Weather radar**: sections as weather systems sweeping across a map.
- 🗝️ **Heist blueprint**: a blueprint floor plan; each room is a step of the plan.
- 🗺️ **Map journey**: real Google Maps / Earth style flights between places for decks about
  geography, travel, offices or markets.
- 🚇 Subway map, 📖 Storybook, 🌊 Timeline river.

## Later
- Business model and pricing.
- Not planned for v1: templates gallery, real-time collaboration, embeds, mobile-optimised links.
